## What's new

- **Match found** — Background matchmaking no longer auto-opens the game; tap the notification or return to the app to join. Match-found alerts (ticks/haptics and shade tile) continue until you open the game screen.
- **Segmented status** — Match-found uses a distinct match spinner and a 20s countdown (not queue elapsed time or in-match clock). Fixed early switch to in-match timer before both players accept.
- **Notifications** — Match-found shade stays high-importance in foreground; heads-up peeks without duplicate notification vibration. Home screen shows match-found state reliably during pre-game lobby.
- **Haptics** — Stronger lobby alert ticks, anchor-aligned 500ms beats, and no duplicate ready bursts.
