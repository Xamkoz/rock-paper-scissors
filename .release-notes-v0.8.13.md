## What's new

- **Match-found on home screen** — With background service enabled, match-found alerts now appear in the status bar while you're on the home screen, not only when the app is backgrounded.
- **High-priority match-found** — Match-found notifications use HIGH-importance channels for lock-screen and status-bar visibility; the foreground-service tile elevates during the lobby alert.
- **Lobby tick fixes** — Match-found ticks are separate from the player clock; fixes double ticks in-game and spurious ticks on reconnect or stale matches.
- **Pick a move timing** — The "Pick a move" prompt waits until the dual-reveal animation finishes before showing.
