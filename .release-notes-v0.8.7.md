## What's new

- **Match screen** — Round history fills the space between the score card and move picker (newest rounds on top, no scroll; as many rows as fit).

- **Match found (background)** — Dedicated high-priority notification with the system default alert sound while the app is in the background; fixes cases where background matchmaking showed no alert.
- **AI player** — Prioritizes live in-match throws over stale intel for repeat/counter picks; tighter hint validation and prompt wording.
