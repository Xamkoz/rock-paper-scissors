## What's new

- **Sound & haptic modes** — Clock control cycles sound+haptic, haptic-only, and off; preference migrates from the old mute toggle.
- **Match clock** — Tick sound and haptic pulses after 5s in the low-time window; round resolution uses move-sound bursts plus one haptic pulse.
- **Pre-game feedback** — Match-found and ready ticks fire from the match-found notification, game screen open, and lobby (system vibrator), not fragile Compose effects.
- **Live score flash** — After a round scores, a 1s gate holds the old win count; the digit snaps with a quick crossfade and a bright color pop (gold for you, accent for opponent).
