## What's new

- **Connection errors** — During a live match, clocks pause when offline instead of ticking against stale state. Firestore listener blips no longer drop the active match; the game resyncs when connection returns.
- **Match timer** — Segmented in-match elapsed time starts when the first live round begins (both players ready), not at match creation.
