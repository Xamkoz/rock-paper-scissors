## What's new

- **Dual reveal gate** — Round recap no longer skips when the opponent picks immediately on the next round; the hold runs to completion even if match state flickers.
- **Round recap timing** — Revealed moves and outcome banner stay up for 1.2 s (was 1.8 s) before the next round UI.
- **In-match clock feedback** — Tick sounds start after 3 s; haptics after 7 s (from when your clock begins).
